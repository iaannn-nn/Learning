# How do you know which concepts you now understand clearly?
- [ ] **Teach it simply:** you can explain it to a child without jargon.
- [ ] **Transfer it:** you can apply it to new problems, not just familiar examples.
- [ ] **Connect it:** you can show how it links to other ideas in the field.
- [ ] **Critique it:** you can spot flawed reasoning and explain why it's wrong.
- [ ] **Know the why:** you understand the purpose behind the process, not just the steps.
