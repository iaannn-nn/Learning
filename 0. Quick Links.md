# How do you know which concepts you now understand clearly?
- [ ] **Teach it simply:** you can explain it to a child without jargon.
- [ ] **Transfer it:** you can apply it to new problems, not just familiar examples.
- [ ] **Connect it:** you can show how it links to other ideas in the field.
- [ ] **Critique it:** you can spot flawed reasoning and explain why it's wrong.
- [ ] **Know the why:** you understand the purpose behind the process, not just the steps.


# Writing Tips
Strunk's rules behind the edit:
- **Use the active voice and strong verbs.** "Are descriptions of" became "describe."
- **Omit needless words.** "That will satisfy" became "to meet."
- **Prefer plain, concrete wording.** "Necessary and sufficient" became "must have, and need only have," which says the same thing in everyday words.

Eg:
- _Requirements_ are descriptions of the necessary and suffi cient properties of a product that will satisfy the consumer’s need.

=> Into bullet point:
- Requirements describe the properties a product must have.
- They include only the properties the product needs.
- Together, these properties meet the consumer's need.
