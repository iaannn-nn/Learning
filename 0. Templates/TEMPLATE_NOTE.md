## Self-Reflection :
- **Identify successes:** Note what you did well and what concepts you now understand clearly.
- **Acknowledge struggles:** Point out specific areas where you felt confused or faced challenges.
- **Analyze reasons:** Think about _why_ you struggled or succeeded with a specific task or topic.
- **Plan next steps:** Write down concrete, actionable changes you can make next time to improve.
