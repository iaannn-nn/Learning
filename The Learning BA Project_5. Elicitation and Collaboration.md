# 5. Elicitation and Collaboration
>  [0.1. README_The Learning BA Project](<0.1. README_The Learning BA Project.md>)

## BABOK
## 1. Prepare for Elicitation
- **Purpose:**
	- to understand the scope of activities necessary to prepare for an elicitation activity, to ensure that the activity will accomplish its desired outcome
- **Goal:**
	- The preparation should:
		- establish a clear understanding of the scope of the elicitation activity
		- select and prepare the appropriate elicitation technique(s)
		- organize logistics, materials, and resources for a successful session
- **Inputs for This Task**
	- Need
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach (from Planning and Monitoring)
- **Elements for This Task**
	- Understand the Scope of the Elicitation Activity
	- Select and Prepare Elicitation Technique(s)
	- Schedule and Set Up Logistics (location, resources, materials)
- **Guideline and Tools**
	- Business Analysis Approach
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach
	- Supporting Materials
- **Techniques**
	- As in BABOK
- **Stakeholder**
	- Domain Expert
	- End User
	- Project Manager
- **Outputs**
	- None formal (elicitation activity plan and logistics)

## 2. Conduct Elicitation
- **Purpose:**
	- to draw out, explore, and identify information relevant to the change from stakeholders and other sources
- **Goal:**
	- The elicitation activity should:
		- guide stakeholders through the selected elicitation technique
		- capture the outcomes of the elicitation activity accurately
- **Inputs for This Task**
	- Elicitation Activity Plan (output of Task 1)
- **Elements for This Task**
	- Guide the Elicitation Activity
	- Capture Outcomes of the Elicitation Activity
- **Guideline and Tools**
	- Business Analysis Approach
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach
- **Techniques**
	- As in BABOK
- **Stakeholder**
	- Domain Expert
	- End User
	- Project Manager
	- Sponsor
- **Outputs**
	- Elicitation Results (unconfirmed)

## 3. Confirm Elicitation Results
- **Purpose:**
	- to check the information gathered during an elicitation session against the source material or stakeholders to ensure accuracy
- **Goal:**
	- The confirmation should:
		- compare documented results against the original source
		- resolve any discrepancies or gaps identified
- **Inputs for This Task**
	- Elicitation Results (unconfirmed, output of Task 2)
- **Elements for This Task**
	- Compare Documented Results Against the Source
	- Resolve Discrepancies/Gaps
- **Guideline and Tools**
	- Information Management Approach
- **Techniques**
	- As in BABOK
- **Stakeholder**
	- Domain Expert
	- Project Manager
- **Outputs**
	- Elicitation Results (confirmed)

## 4. Communicate BA Information
- **Purpose:**
	- to ensure stakeholders have a shared understanding of business analysis information
- **Goal:**
	- The communication should:
		- determine the objectives and appropriate format for the communication
		- package the information in a way that enables stakeholders to use it effectively
		- deliver the communication to the intended audience
- **Inputs for This Task**
	- BA Information (elicitation results, requirements, designs)
- **Elements for This Task**
	- Determine Objectives and Format of Communication
	- Prepare the Communication Package
	- Deliver the Communication
- **Guideline and Tools**
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach
	- Information Management Approach
- **Techniques**
	- As in BABOK
- **Stakeholder**
	- All Stakeholders
- **Outputs**
	- BA Information (communicated)

## 5. Manage Stakeholder Collaboration
- **Purpose:**
	- to encourage stakeholders to work towards a common goal
- **Goal:**
	- The collaboration effort should:
		- gain agreement on commitments from stakeholders
		- monitor ongoing stakeholder engagement and relationships
		- manage stakeholder expectations and resolve conflicts as they arise
- **Inputs for This Task**
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach (from Planning and Monitoring)
- **Elements for This Task**
	- Gain Agreement on Commitments
	- Monitor Stakeholder Engagement
	- Manage Stakeholder Expectations / Conflict Resolution
- **Guideline and Tools**
	- Stakeholder Engagement Approach
	- Governance Approach
- **Techniques**
	- As in BABOK
- **Stakeholder**
	- All Stakeholders
- **Outputs**
	- None formal (ongoing collaboration and engagement)
