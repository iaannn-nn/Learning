## The BA Step-by-Step 
# Step 1: Planning and Monitoring
- **First of all**, we need to have a plan and have a method to monitor our own BA Work when we are assigned to a project 
- ==(Refer to Software Jogger's Chapter 8)== and [The Learning BA Project_4. Planning and Monitoring](<The Learning BA Project_4. Planning and Monitoring.md>). 
	- This step depends on project types and project characteristics.
	- The task we need to do at this step are:
		- **Document our approach to conduct our own business analysis.*==(BA Approach)
			1. Planning Approach: predictive or adaptive
			2. Formality and Detail Level of BA Deliverables
			3. BA Activities
			4. Timing of BA Work
		- **Document our plan to engage with the stakeholders.==(Stakeholder Engagement Plan)**
			1. Perform Stakeholder Analysis :
				1. Identify Stakeholders
				2. Conduct Stakeholder Analysis (influence, authority level, attitude, complexity)
			2. Define Stakeholder Collaboration
			3. Determine Stakeholder Engagement Approach (how/when to communicate with each stakeholder)
		- **Document our plan to govern our own business analysis==(Business Analysis Governance)** ==
			1. Decision Making: who is involved, and the process used (consensus, authoritative, voting)
			2. Change Control Process: how proposed changes to requirements/designs are assessed and approved
			3. Plan Business Analysis Information Management Process (linkage to Task 4)
			4. Prioritization Process: criteria used to prioritize requirements and designs
			5. Business Analysis Deliverable Approval Process: who signs off and when
		- **Document our plan to management information of our own business analysis==(Business Analysis Information Management)**
			1. Information Organization: how requirements are structured, grouped, or categorized
			2. Level of Abstraction: the appropriate amount of detail for the audience
			3. Plan for Requirements Reuse: traceability to support reuse across initiatives
			4. Storage and Access: tools, repositories, and access rights
			5. Requirements Attributes: metadata tracked for each requirement (e.g. status, priority, source)
		- **Document our method to assess our own business analysis work and recommend actionable improvements.( Business Analysis Performance)** 
			1. Performance Analysis: compare planned vs. actual BA performance
			2. Assessment Measures: metrics and KPIs used to measure BA performance
			3. Identify Performance Issues and Root Causes
			4. Recommend Actions for Improvement
	- How we gonna do all the tasks?
		- 1. Identify the project types 
		- Depends on the Project Types, 
			- we need to list all the bullet points as i**n the book pdf page 305/370 's suggested analysis models and requirements-related documentation**
			- There are only 6 types of software development projects in total : **New Development, Maintenance( Enhancement, Correction, Adaptation), Commercial Off-The-Shelf.** 
			- Depends on the types, the book suggested different set of analysis models and requirement documentations, which is our ==BA Approach's item 2, BA Deliverable==
			- The Formality, Detail Level, and Management of those models and documentations depends on the project characteristics in the next step ==(still BA Approach's item 2)==
	- 2. Identify the project characteristics: risk or change driven
		- This characteristic and other project factors will decide the most apt requirement practices
		- To select the right practices, you need to ask 3 key questions:
			- Who are the consumers of the reqs?
			- What will the requirements be used for?
			- Where does this project fall in the range of factors that characterize a project
		- How to answer this 3 questions:
			- 1. Evaluate the project characteristics
			- 2. Select the elicitation techniques, analysis models, requirements validation techniques, and management practices for the project
			- 3. Determine the necessary requirements works products.
